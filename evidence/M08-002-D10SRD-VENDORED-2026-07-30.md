## Reclassification

M08-001 recorded this as a documentation gap and left Loop 6 PARTIAL. That was the wrong
diagnosis, corrected by the principal:

> this isn't a gap in documentation. it's an orphan. somewhere along the lines one of you
> guys just stripped out load bearing scaffolding and never mentioned it.

That is the accurate reading, and it changes what the fix has to be. The rules were never
missing from the ecosystem — `d10SRD` is its own repository, its rules are executable
JavaScript, and their licence explicitly permits distribution. The scaffolding that
connected them to the product that must conform to them was removed, and nothing recorded
its removal. What filled the hole was a transcription of the numbers into a test, which
cannot fail for the right reason: it asserts a copy against itself.

An orphan is not repaired by documenting the hole more carefully. It is repaired by
restoring the connection.

Earlier d10 SRD material released under CC0 remains CC0; that grant is not withdrawn or
relicensed. Attribution: d10SRD, Copyright 2026 Rusty Cohl and contributors.

**`vendor/d10srd/PROVENANCE.json` (new)** — upstream repository, exact commit
`c77005b20d74d25a426e6b7620e63d49b0778105`, clean-tree note at vendoring time, a `sha256`
per file, the licensing note, and the update procedure. The procedure is written so a
partial update cannot pass: digests and both version fields must move together.

**`tests/d10srd-conformance.test.mjs` (new)** — imports the vendored rules and executes them
against all six published vectors.

| Vector | How it is now checked |
|---|---|
| 1 — ability table | full 4–20 table through `abilityModifier`, including the `4–5 → −2` row the SRD calls out because naive halving loses it; refusal outside the integer 4–20 domain |
| 2 — DC conversion | `scaleD20DC` over the published 5/10/15/20/25 → 3/5/8/10/13 |
| 3 — threat confirmation | `resolveCheck` on natural 1 and 10: 6 confirms, 5 does not, both asserted; a threat with no confirmation supplied is refused rather than defaulted; an ordinary roll carries no confirmation |
| 4 — skill ranks | accepted at 0/1/5/9/10, refused at −1, 11, 2.5 |
| 5 — AP maximum | cross-checked across all three surfaces that state it: the vendored document, `GameConfig.MAX_AP` parsed out of the Godot source, and the Godot-side pin |
| 6 — authority boundary | the "do not divide health, damage, movement…" sentence must still be present in the vendored document |

Vector 6 deserves a note. That sentence is the only thing justifying the split between
`_test_d10_conformance` and `_test_balance_baseline` on the Godot side. If it ever leaves
the SRD, the split stops being grounded in anything, and that has to be a visible event
rather than a silent one.

**`game/data/d10srd_conformance.json` (updated)** — gains a `vendored_authority` block
naming the path, the provenance file, the executing test, and the three digests. Vectors 1–4
stay `applies_to_godot_port: false`, which remains true — this port implements no check
resolution — but their reasons now record that the vector is exercised against the vendored
rules rather than merely excused. The `forbidden_symbols` guard stays: it is what catches the
port growing a second resolver.

The two sides can no longer drift apart. The test asserts the pin's `rules_id` and
`srd_version` equal the vendored provenance, so neither can be re-pinned alone.
